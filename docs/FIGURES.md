# Mapping to the revised Results section

The ten PDFs in recorded_figures/ are the saved figure files, not new plots.

## Main Results figures

- results_policy_comparison.pdf: eight policies and descriptive paired intervals.
- results_fullgrid_validation.pdf: minimax contrasts with fixed and balanced rules.
- results_cooperative_attribution.pdf: development-fitted Shapley contributions and interactions.
- results_observation_pricing.pdf: catalogue, observation and interference-pricing comparisons.
- results_fullgrid_transfer.pdf: frozen minimax versus parameter-pair transfer scores.
- results_coalition_transfer.pdf: frozen coalition-policy scores, not new coalition worths.

## Supplementary figures

- results_cost_reliability.pdf: actual expenditure, not nominal budget caps.
- supp_validation_secondary.pdf: extraction-weighted validation contrasts.
- supp_sensitivity_secondary.pdf: extraction-weighted sensitivity contrasts.
- supp_catalogue_expansion.pdf: full-grid original-to-augmented losses.

The first plotting script generates the five representative figures and an older
combined results_supplement.pdf. The second generates the promoted full-grid
figures and current supplementary grids. Use the mapping above for the revised
manuscript. The older combined PDF is not a replacement for the revised
supplementary manuscript. Figure numbers are assigned by the manuscript's LaTeX,
not hard-coded here. No manuscript PDF or confidential submission is included.
